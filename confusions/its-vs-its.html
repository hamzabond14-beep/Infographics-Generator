Its vs It's

Quick answer

Its shows that something belongs to “it” (no apostrophe). It's is a contraction of “it is” or “it has” (always with an apostrophe).

Its: shows possession

Use its (no apostrophe) when something belongs to “it” — an object, animal, or idea.

✓ The company updated its logo.
✕ The company updated it's logo.
✓ The dog wagged its tail.
✕ The dog wagged it's tail.

It's: short for “it is” or “it has”

Use it's (with an apostrophe) only when you mean “it is” or “it has.”

✓ It's raining again.
✕ Its raining again.
✓ It's been a long week.
✕ Its been a long week.

Why this one trips people up

Normally an apostrophe + s shows possession (“Sarah's book”). Its is the exception — possessive pronouns like his, hers, and its never take an apostrophe.

Memory trick

Expand it: “it's” always means “it is” or “it has.” If that swap doesn't work, drop the apostrophe and use its.
