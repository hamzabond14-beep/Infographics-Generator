Accept vs Except

Quick answer

Accept means to receive, agree to, or approve of something. Except means excluding or leaving something out.

Accept: to receive or agree to

Use accept when someone agrees to, receives, or approves of something.

✓ She decided to accept the job offer.
✕ She decided to except the job offer.
✓ We accept all major credit cards.
✕ We except all major credit cards.

Except: excluding something

Use except when you're leaving something out of a group or statement.

✓ Everyone came to the party except Sam.
✕ Everyone came to the party accept Sam.
✓ The store is open every day except Sunday.
✕ The store is open every day accept Sunday.

A helpful contrast

Accept is about inclusion — letting something in. Except is about exclusion — leaving something out. Thinking of them as opposites makes the choice easy.

Memory trick

Accept starts like “access” — letting something in. Except starts like “exclude” — leaving something out.

Related word confusions

AffectvsEffect

Affect is (almost always) a verb meaning “to influence.” Effect is (almost always) a noun meaning “a result.” If you can swap in “influence,” use affect. If you can swap in “a result,” use effect.

ThenvsThan

Then is about time or what happens next (“first this, then that”). Than is used to compare two things (“better than,” “more than”).
