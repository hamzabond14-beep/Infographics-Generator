Word confusion

Their vs There vs They're

Quick answer

Their shows ownership (“their car”). There refers to a place or existence (“over there,” “there is”). They're is a contraction of “they are.”

Their: shows possession

Use their when something belongs to a group of people or things.

✓ The students left their books on the desk.
✕ The students left there books on the desk.
✓ That's their decision to make.
✕ That's they're decision to make.

There: refers to a place or introduces a statement

Use there to point to a location, or to introduce that something exists (“there is,” “there are”).

✓ Put the box over there.
✕ Put the box over their.
✓ There are three options available.
✕ Their are three options available.

They're: short for “they are”

Use they're only when you could substitute “they are” without changing the meaning.

✓ They're coming over for dinner tonight.
✕ There coming over for dinner tonight.
✓ I think they're right about this.
✕ I think their right about this.
Memory trick

If you can replace the word with “they are” and the sentence still works, it's they're. If you're pointing somewhere, it's there (notice “there” contains “here”). Otherwise, it's probably their.
