The problem

What is a “word confusion”?

English is full of words that look alike, sound alike, or simply get mixed up in the moment — even by fluent speakers and confident writers.

Homophones

Words that sound identical but differ in spelling and meaning, like their, there, and they're.

Near-synonyms

Words with overlapping meanings that aren't quite interchangeable, like affect and effect.

Look-alikes

Words that share letters and a similar shape but different jobs, like its and it's.
